Our Story

A kitchen built on patience and produce.

Chef Édouard Leroux returned from Lyon in 1998 with a single ambition: to cook the kind of food that made his grandmother leave the table reluctantly. No theatre, no foam. Just honest technique and ingredients worth travelling for.

Our suppliers are named on the menu. The heirloom tomatoes come from Gilles Cornu in Provence. The cheeses are selected weekly by Maître Fromager Sabine Aubert. The wine list is built around small-domaine Burgundy and Alsatian whites, with a particular fondness for producers who farm biodynamically.

A meal here is unhurried. We hope you will feel that immediately.
